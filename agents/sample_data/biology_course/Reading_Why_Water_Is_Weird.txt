Why Water Is Weird (short reading)

Most substances get denser when they freeze, but water does the opposite. As water cools below 4 degrees Celsius, hydrogen bonds lock molecules into an open crystal, so ice floats. That floating layer insulates lakes in winter, which lets fish survive under the ice. Water's high specific heat also means coastal cities have milder temperatures than inland ones.
