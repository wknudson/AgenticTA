# Unit 4 notes: Mendel and inheritance

Gregor Mendel bred pea plants and found that traits come from pairs of factors we now call alleles.

- Dominant alleles hide recessive ones (capital vs lowercase letters).
- Genotype is the allele pair (Tt); phenotype is the trait you see (tall).
- Homozygous: TT or tt. Heterozygous: Tt.
- Law of segregation: allele pairs separate when gametes form.
