# Unit 1 notes: the four macromolecules

| Macromolecule | Monomer | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Carbohydrates | monosaccharides (glucose) | quick energy, structure (cellulose) |
| Lipids | fatty acids and glycerol | long-term energy, cell membranes |
| Proteins | amino acids | enzymes, structure, transport |
| Nucleic acids | nucleotides | store and pass on genetic information (DNA, RNA) |

Dehydration synthesis joins monomers by removing water. Hydrolysis breaks polymers apart by adding water.
