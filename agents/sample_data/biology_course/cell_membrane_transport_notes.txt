Unit 2 notes: the cell membrane and transport

The membrane is a phospholipid bilayer with proteins floating in it (fluid mosaic model). It is selectively permeable.

Passive transport needs no energy: diffusion, osmosis (water moving across a membrane), facilitated diffusion through channel proteins.
Active transport uses ATP to move things against the concentration gradient, like the sodium-potassium pump.
Hypotonic solution: cell gains water and swells. Hypertonic: cell loses water and shrinks. Isotonic: no net change.
